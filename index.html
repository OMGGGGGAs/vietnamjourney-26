Arrival & Old Quarter Immersion

Overnight international flight. Sleep as much as you can — you land at dawn and the city won't let you rest long.

05:30–08:00

🛬 Immigration + Hotel Drop

Clear customs, grab a Grab (not a metered taxi!), drop bags at hotel. Check in if room is ready.

Don't get into unlicensed taxis at Noi Bai — look for the official Grab pickup zone

08:00–09:00

Even 45 mins horizontal will help. Power through — the jet lag is worth it.

The Must-Do Experience

EGG COFFEE
HIT LIST ☕

Invented in 1946 when milk was scarce during the French War, cà phê trứng is Hanoi's most iconic drink — whisked egg yolk, condensed milk, and strong robusta coffee. Creamy, rich, dessert-like. Try at least two spots to compare.

Café Giang

39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân, Old Quarter

The original since 1946. Jensen Huang, John Legend & Antony Blinken have all sat in this tiny upstairs room. Unchanged recipe for 80 years.

The Birthplace

Café Dinh

13 Đinh Tiên Hoàng, 2nd Floor

Opened by Mr. Giang's daughter. 2nd-floor hideaway overlooking Hoan Kiem Lake. Raw, soulful, and the most emotional egg coffee you'll have.

Lake View Gem

Café Pho Co

11 Hàng Gai (behind the silk shop)

Hidden behind a silk shop — climb three floors to a stunning rooftop terrace with Hoan Kiem views. The most cinematic egg coffee moment in Hanoi.

Rooftop Secret

Café Nang

Near Nguyễn Hữu Huân

Try the iced version — perfect for June heat. The coconut egg coffee variation is worth ordering on its own merit.

Try It Iced

Things Most Tourists Miss

LOCAL SECRETS

The stuff that turns a good trip into an unforgettable one.

01
🌅

Hoan Kiem at Dawn (5:30am)

Before tourists arrive, the lake belongs to locals doing tai chi and morning exercises. The red bridge glows. Worth the 5am wake-up just once.

Hanoi · Hidden Timing
02
🚗

The Sleeper Bus Hack

Book Sapa Friendly Bus or Techbus. Bottom bunk at the front. Earplugs, neck pillow, blanket. You wake up in the mountains — genuinely magic.

Transport · Sapa Route
03
🎵

Ca Trù — Ancient Chamber Music

UNESCO intangible heritage. Female vocalists perform 15th-century court music in temple rooms. Completely unlike anything else.

Hanoi · Culture
04
🧧

Đồng Xuân at 6am

Wholesale flower vendors, live poultry, fresh produce — all gone by 9am. The most alive thing you'll see in Hanoi.

Hanoi · Early Morning
05
💊

Vietnamese Pharmacy Culture

Pharmacies are everywhere and incredibly cheap. Tell the pharmacist your symptoms, sorted under $3. Stock up on Tiger Balm post-Sapa trek.

Practical · All Cities
06
🍃

Trà Đá — Vietnam's Free Tea

Iced green tea is given free at almost every street food stall in Vietnam. Never pay for it. Drink it constantly in the June heat.

Food · All Vietnam
07
📺

Water Puppet Theatre

Thăng Long near Hoan Kiem Lake. 1,000-year-old art form, puppets on a water stage, live traditional music. 45 mins. Strange and mesmerising.

Hanoi · Experience
08
🛵

Cross the Street Like a Local

Don't wait for a gap — it won't come. Walk slowly and steadily. Motorbikes will flow around you. Stop suddenly and you'll get hit. Slow = safe.

Survival · All Vietnam
09
🌿

Ask for Cốm on Hàng Than St

Young green sticky rice from West Lake fields — Hanoi's most seasonal delicacy. June isn't peak but ask around. If you find it, eat it with ripe banana.

Hanoi · Seasonal

The Complete Eat List

EAT YOUR WAY THROUGH

Every city, every must-order dish, every spot that matters.

Phở Gia Truyền Bát Đàn

49 Bát Đàn

Cash only, no menu, queue out the door. North Vietnamese beef pho — crystal clear broth, thin sliced beef. The real thing.

Must: Phở Tái (rare beef)

Bún Chả Đắc Kim

1 Hàng Mành

Chargrilled pork + cold vermicelli. One of Hanoi's oldest bún chả spots. Exceptional smokiness on the pork.

Must: Bún Chả + Nem Cua Bể

Chả Cá Lã Vọng

14 Chả Cá

Since 1871. Tableside turmeric fish with dill. One dish only. The most uniquely Hanoian meal you'll have.

Must: The Only Dish

Bánh Cuốn Thanh Vân

14 Hàng Gà

Hand-pulled steamed rice rolls made fresh in front of you. Pork, mushroom, fried shallots. Silky and delicate.

Must: Bánh Cuốn Nhân Thịt

Bún Ốc Quán Bà Nga

61 Trúc Bạch, Tây Hồ

Sour tamarind-tomato broth with river snails and fried tofu. A true Hanoian specialty. Locals only.

Must: Bún Ốc Chua

Xôi Yến

35B Nguyễn Hữu Huân

Hanoi's most famous sticky rice stall. Open 6am, sold out by 9am. Pâté, sausage, egg, fried shallots on top.

Must: Xôi Xéo (mung bean)

Bếp Sapa

Đường Cầu Mây

Actual H'Mong-style cooking — not adjusted for tourists. Black bean braised pork, thắng cố stew.

Must: Sườn Hầm Măng

La Sa Pa Restaurant

55 Fansipan Rd

Sapa salmon hotpot. Mountain-stream fish, clean herby broth. Best dinner in Sapa, full stop.

Must: Lẩu Cá Hồi Sapa

Hill Station Signature

7 Muong Hoa St

Elevated H'Mong-inspired cuisine. Best plating in Sapa. Worth the splurge for one evening.

Must: Chef's Tasting Set

On-Deck Cruise Lunch

Sunlight Grand Cruise

Tiger prawns, steamed clams, morning glory. All same-day caught. Eat with 1,600 karsts behind your plate.

Must: Grilled Prawns + Clams

On-Deck Bia Hoi

Sunlight Grand Cruise Bar

Fresh draft beer on the cruise deck. Cheap, cold, and infinitely satisfying with that view.

Order: Early, Often

Hủ Tiếu Nam Vang

Various locations, District 5

HCMC's pho equivalent — clear pork broth, flat rice noodles, shrimp, quail egg. Southern style: sweeter, more complex.

Must: Hủ Tiếu Khô

Bánh Xèo 46A

46A Đinh Công Tráng, D1

Sizzling Vietnamese crepe. Giant crispy pancake with shrimp and bean sprouts. Wrap in rice paper, dip in nước chấm.

Must: Bánh Xèo

Bánh Mì Huynh Hoa

26 Lê Thị Riêng, D1

Often called Saigon's best bánh mì. Enormous, overstuffed, incredibly crispy. Queue from 9am, worth every minute.

Must: Đặc Biệt (Special)
